01Project overview

One request. The right work, in the right order.

The Catalog Item collects organization, department, role type, and specific access requirements. After submission, Flow Designer branches into Vendor A, Vendor B, and internal staff paths.

Each path creates a placeholder user and routes to IT fulfillment. Vendor routes trigger tasks or sub-items; the internal route processes setup. The paths converge for notifications and onboarding completion.

02How it works

A clear route from request to completion.

  1. 01

    Capture the request

    Select an organization, department, and role type, then enter access needs.

  2. 02

    Branch by requester

    Route the submission to Vendor A, Vendor B, or internal staff.

  3. 03

    Coordinate setup

    Create a placeholder user, route to IT fulfillment, and initiate vendor tasks, sub-items, or internal setup.

  4. 04

    Bring the work together

    Converge the paths to send notifications and complete onboarding.
